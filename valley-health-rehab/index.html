About Us

Rooted in the Bitterroot. Focused on you.

The Valley Health and Rehab is a 58-bed skilled nursing and rehabilitation center at 601 N 10th St in Hamilton, part of The Goodman Group family of care communities. We are certified by both Medicare and Medicaid.

Placeholder copyOur team helps each resident recover, regain independence and live with dignity, with views of the Bitterroot Mountains and the close-knit feel of a small Montana town.

Our Services

The right care, right here in Hamilton

From a short stay after a hospital visit to a long-term home, our team is here around the clock.

Skilled Nursing

Licensed nurses providing medication management, wound care, post-surgical care and support for complex medical needs.

Short-Term Rehabilitation

Therapy after a hospital stay, surgery, stroke or injury, with one goal: helping you get safely back home.

Long-Term Care

A safe, comfortable place to live, with daily support, meals, activities and caring staff who get to know each resident.

Rehabilitation & Therapy

Stronger every day. Home sooner.

Our rehab program is built around your goals, whether that's walking to the mailbox, getting back to the river, or simply going home safely.

  • Physical therapy: strength, balance and walking
  • Occupational therapy: everyday living skills
  • Speech therapy: swallowing and communication
  • Discharge planning with you and your family
