Our Services

The right care, right here in Hamilton

From a short stay after surgery to a long-term home, we're here around the clock.

24-Hour Skilled Nursing

Licensed nurses on site day and night for medication management, wound care, post-surgical needs and complex medical care.

Short-Term Rehabilitation

Therapy after a hospital stay, surgery, stroke or injury, with one goal: getting you safely back home.

Long-Term Care

A warm, safe place to live with daily support, meals, activities and caring staff who become like family.

Rehabilitation & Therapy

Stronger every day. Home sooner.

Our rehab program is built around your goals, whether that's walking to the mailbox, getting back to fishing the Bitterroot, or simply going home safely.

  • Physical therapy: strength, balance and walking
  • Occupational therapy: daily living skills
  • Speech therapy: swallowing and communication
  • Discharge planning with your family
