Meet Racheal

Racheal Allen became a licensed REALTOR® in 2019 and joined Homes for Heroes through Coldwell Banker in 2020, giving back financially to military members, law enforcement, firefighters, healthcare workers and teachers.

Before real estate, she volunteered as a Police Community Advocate, helping bridge the gap between the community and law enforcement.
