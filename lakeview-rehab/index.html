About Lakeview

A beautiful place to heal, recover, and call home.

Located in picturesque Bigfork, Montana, Lakeview Rehab & Nursing Care Center offers a unique combination of professional nursing care, personalized rehabilitation, and the peaceful beauty of the Flathead Valley. Perched on a hillside overlooking the magnificent waters of Flathead Lake, our facility provides a scenic setting where residents can focus on healing, recovery, and quality of life.

“Exceptional care begins with treating every resident as an individual.”

Our interdisciplinary team works together to address each resident’s medical, physical, emotional, and social needs while promoting independence, dignity, and meaningful connections. Whether someone joins us for short-term rehabilitation following a hospitalization or requires ongoing long-term nursing care, our mission is to provide a safe, supportive, and welcoming environment for residents and their families.

Our Services

Care shaped around each person.

From recovery after a hospital stay to long-term nursing support, every plan is built around a resident’s needs, abilities, preferences, and goals.

Short-Term Rehabilitation

For individuals recovering from surgery, hospitalization, illness, or injury, our rehabilitation-focused approach is designed to support recovery, improve mobility, and help residents work toward their highest practical level of independence.

Contact Our Team

Skilled Nursing Care

Our nursing team provides individualized care for residents requiring ongoing medical monitoring, nursing interventions, medication management, and assistance with daily living.

Long-Term Residential Care

Ongoing nursing care and daily support for individuals who require a higher level of assistance than can be safely provided at home.

Physical Therapy

Individualized rehabilitation programs focused on improving strength, mobility, balance, endurance, and functional independence.

Occupational Therapy

Therapeutic interventions focused on improving the ability to complete everyday activities, including dressing, bathing, personal care, and other essential daily tasks.

Speech Therapy

Therapy services addressing communication, cognition, and swallowing difficulties when clinically appropriate.

Individualized Care Planning

Our interdisciplinary team develops and reviews personalized care plans based on each resident’s medical needs, functional abilities, preferences, and personal goals.

Social Services & Resident Support

Support for residents and families through care coordination, adjustment to facility living, discharge planning, and access to community resources.

Activities & Social Engagement

Opportunities for meaningful recreation, social interaction, and activities that support residents’ emotional well-being and quality of life.

Dining & Nutritional Support

Meal services and nutritional care designed to support residents’ dietary needs, preferences, and personal goals.
